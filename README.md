# Uncertainty-Aware-Knowledge-Distillation-for-Semi-Supervised-3D-MRI-Segmentation
Existing semi-supervised 3D MRI segmentation methods often suffer from unreliable pseudo-labels, limited boundary modeling with standard convolutions, and insufficient representation of complex anatomical structures under scarce labeled data. To address these issues, we propose a semi-supervised framework with a dual-branch teacher–student architecture that integrates pseudo-label enhancement and consistency regularization. It introduces three key components: (1) progressive uncertainty-aware distillation that suppresses unreliable pseudo-label propagation; (2) attention-guided 3D deformable convolution that improves feature extraction and irregular boundary modeling within regions of interest while preserving computational efficiency; and (3) a unified semi-supervised optimization strategy that synergistically combines these components. Experiments on the LA and BraTS19 datasets show that the proposed method achieves performance comparable to fully supervised baselines with minimal labeled data and consistently outperforms state-of-the-art semi-supervised methods. With only 20% labeled data on LA, it attains a Dice score of 91.88%, just 0.8 percentage points below the fully supervised baseline, substantially reducing annotation requirements while improving robustness through uncertainty mitigation.
